I'm **Prince Kumar** — a third-year B.Tech Bioinformatics student at **SASTRA Deemed University**,
working at the crossroads of biology, data, and imagination.

Most weeks I'm in a Linux shell, pushing gut metagenome reads through QC, host filtering and
taxonomic profiling, and asking uncomfortable questions about how much of a "result" is really
just the reference database talking. The rest of the time I'm rendering 3D for my college's
design club, or out with a camera waiting for the light to do something interesting.

I like work that is quiet, careful, and reproducible — and I like it to *look* like something.

### 🧬 Reference database choice changes who you think lives in the gut

**First author · with N.T. Devika · Dept. of Bioinformatics, SASTRA · poster @ AIxLIFE 2026**

SILVA and Greengenes disagree about species-level assignment more than people admit. I built
**SG_consensus**, a merged reference that reduces database-dependent variability, then compared
microbial co-occurrence networks (SparCC, |r| ≥ 0.5, p < 0.01) across all three references on a
host–pathogen infection model. Database choice measurably shifts the ecological story you end up
telling. Conflict resolution is partial; refinement ongoing.

`QIIME 2` · `SparCC` · `SILVA` · `Greengenes` · `16S rRNA`
